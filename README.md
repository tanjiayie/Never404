## 💡 The Problem

University students rarely struggle with just **one** responsibility.

Assignments, classes, part-time work, social commitments, errands, and personal wellbeing can all compete for the same limited **time and energy**.

The problem is not simply:

> **"I have too many tasks."**

It is:

> **"I don't realise how everything together is affecting me — or what I should change."**

Existing apps usually focus on only one side:

| App             | Main Focus                  | What's Missing?               |
| --------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 🌱 **Finch**    | Self-care & virtual pet     | Overall workload management   |
| 🎮 **Habitica** | Productivity & gamification | Emotional state & recovery    |
| 💬 **Wysa**     | AI emotional support        | Practical workload management |
| ✨ **LumiCare**  | **Workload + wellbeing**    | —                             |

# ✨ Our Solution

**LumiCare** is an AI-powered companion that connects **workload management with wellbeing**.

Instead of simply helping students complete more tasks, LumiCare helps them understand their overall capacity and make more sustainable decisions.

### 🔄 The Core Flow

```text
💬 Talk
   ↓
🧠 Understand
   ↓
📊 Analyse Load
   ↓
⚠️ Identify Pressure
   ↓
⚖️ Rebalance
   ↓
🌿 Recover
   ↓
✨ Reflect & Progress
```

# 🐻 Meet Lumi

Lumi is more than a decorative mascot.

The companion reflects the user's workload and recovery journey.

| State             | What Happens                                                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 😊 **Happy**      | Workload is manageable                                       |
| 😴 **Tired**      | Workload becomes too high                                    |
| 🌿 **Recovering** | User starts taking recovery actions                          |
| ✨ **Recharged**   | User maintains recovery and completes the recovery challenge |

This makes the user's wellbeing **visible and interactive**.

# 🚀 Key Features

### 💬 AI Companion Chat

Talk naturally about your day, assignments, commitments, emotions, or anything contributing to your workload.

### 🧠 AI Context Understanding

Lumi extracts useful information from conversations, such as:

* Tasks
* Deadlines
* Classes
* Work commitments
* Social activities
* Emotional context

### 📊 Multi-Dimensional Workload

Lumi looks beyond the number of tasks and considers different areas:

**Mental · Time · Physical · Social · Everyday Life**

This gives users a broader picture of their overall workload.

### ⚠️ Pressure Point Identification

Lumi identifies which responsibilities are contributing most to the user's current pressure.

Instead of only asking:

> *"What do I need to finish?"*

Lumi also asks:

> *"What's contributing most to my current load?"*

### ⚖️ Interactive Rebalancing

Users can adjust commitments and immediately see the **projected impact on their workload**.

This allows them to explore different options before making decisions.

### 🌿 Recovery Mode

Recovery is treated as part of workload management — not something that only happens after everything is finished.

Recovery actions include:

* 🫁 Deep Breath
* 🌳 Nature Walk
* 🛌 Proper Rest
* 💬 Talk to Someone
* ☕ Me Time

### ✨ Sparks & Rewards

Users earn **Sparks** by completing recovery activities.

Sparks can then be used to customise Lumi with items such as:

🎀 Ribbon · 🧢 Cap · 🛋️ Sofa · 🌱 Plant · 💡 Lamp · 👕 Outfit

### 🔥 3-Day Recovery Challenge

Users are encouraged to complete recovery activities consistently for three days.

Completing the challenge helps Lumi progress towards its **Recharged** state.

### ❤️ Heart-Rate Insights

The MVP includes **simulated heart-rate data** to demonstrate how physiological signals could complement workload and emotional information.

Real smartwatch integration is planned as a future enhancement.

### 🔔 Gentle Reminders

Lumi provides gentle reminders that encourage users to return for daily reflection, recovery, or workload check-ins — without adding another source of pressure.

# 🔐 AI & Security Architecture

AI requests are handled through a **Supabase Edge Function** rather than directly from the frontend.

```text
User
 ↓
React Frontend
 ↓
Supabase Edge Function
 ↓
Google Gemini API
 ↓
Structured AI Response
 ↓
Workload Engine
 ↓
LumiCare UI
```

This approach helps prevent the Gemini API key from being exposed on the client side and provides a server-side layer for:

* 🔐 Authentication
* 🛡️ Request validation
* ⏱️ Timeout handling
* 🚦 Rate-limit handling
* 📊 AI usage monitoring
